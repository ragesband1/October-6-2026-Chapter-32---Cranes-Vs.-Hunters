Chapter 32: Cranes Vs. Hunters

Written by Edgar Allan Walker And "The Rages Band"

Backstory:

Once upon a time, deep in the jungle of the birds, lived a flock of whooping cranes. For 30 years they thrived in harmony, laying their precious eggs atop high tree canopy nests.

However, cruel strangers known as Hunters invaded the forest. They ruthlessly targeted young cranes—whose soft skin is delicate pink before growing into snow-white adult plumage—and stole unhatched eggs.

Filled with sorrow and courage, the adult cranes formed an alliance with good human conservationists at the Orphanage Cage Sanctuary. Now, as a Guardian Crane, you must fly into the jungle, rescue pink chicks, retrieve stolen eggs, and call down the Authorities to put the hunters behind bars forever!

Moral Lesson: Respect and care for our animal nature. Save the animals to protect our world so no creature's domain is destroyed.
